# NAXUS AI

**Autonomous AI Business Intelligence & Operations Platform**

NAXUS AI is an independent software project focused on building a modular platform that helps people research opportunities, coordinate AI agents, and automate repeatable business workflows with clear human oversight.

## Vision

Create a reliable AI operations platform that supports business research, planning, customer workflows, and operational intelligence—while keeping people in control of consequential decisions.

## Core Capabilities

- **Business Intelligence:** Research markets, organize signals, and summarize potential opportunities.
- **AI Agent Orchestration:** Coordinate specialized agents with explicit roles, tools, and boundaries.
- **Workflow Automation:** Build observable, repeatable workflows for routine operations.
- **Customer Operations:** Assist with customer inquiries and support processes.
- **Revenue Intelligence:** Track business activity, costs, and revenue indicators.
- **Human Approval:** Require authorization before external, financial, sensitive, or irreversible actions.

## Principles

1. **Independent project:** NAXUS AI maintains its own codebase, configuration, data, and deployment.
2. **Human control:** No consequential action without the required user approval.
3. **Security first:** Keep credentials out of source control and protect user and customer data.
4. **Transparency:** Make agent actions, errors, and workflow outcomes inspectable.
5. **Evidence-based results:** Measure performance; do not claim revenue or successful automation without evidence.
6. **Practical infrastructure:** Prefer free-tier-friendly services where feasible, while documenting their limits.

## Safety and Scope

NAXUS AI is a software platform under development. It does not guarantee income, customers, or business outcomes. AI-generated research and recommendations may be incomplete or incorrect and should be reviewed. External communications, purchases, payments, account changes, and other consequential operations must remain behind explicit authorization and appropriate safeguards.

## Development

Development instructions will be added as the application stack and local setup are established.

Never commit API keys, passwords, access tokens, private customer data, or production secrets. Use environment variables and a local, untracked environment file.
